Our Mission

Education as a right,
not a privilege.

Damma was born from a belief that those who show resilience, distinction, and ambition deserve access to meaningful opportunities.

We serve Egypt's middle class — talented individuals whose potential is limited only by financial constraints. Every applicant is thoroughly vetted for dedication, integrity, perseverance, and commitment.

Beyond financial support, Damma provides guidance and mentorship — ensuring every contribution is impactful, personal, and human.

The Journey

How a dream becomes reality.

1

Online Application

Applicants submit their story and goals through the Damma portal.

2

Vetting

Each applicant is assessed through interviews and references for character, dedication, and integrity.

3

Mentoring

Career guidance and a personalised training path are crafted for each candidate.

4

Dream Upload

The story is uploaded anonymously on the platform with the training plan and funding needs.

5

Matchmaking

A sponsor browses the platform and directly funds the dream that resonates with them.

6

Training Begins

Funds are released directly to the educator, and the candidate begins their programme.

7

Progress Tracking

Sponsors receive regular updates on completion and real-world outcomes.
